Abstract

Student mental wellness monitoring in academic institutions typically relies on single-metric self-reporting, which is prone to manipulation, data sparsity, and oscillation artifacts. This paper presents AURA (An Adaptive AI System for Emotional and Academic Growth), a comprehensive institutional AI-driven student mental wellness and academic support platform.

AURA is a multi-signal behavioral stress estimation framework that fuses six heterogeneous signals—self-reported mood, conversational sentiment, engagement activity, emotional volatility, temporal context, and trend momentum—through adaptive weight redistribution and exponential moving average stabilization.

Evaluation demonstrates 92% variance reduction, 22% manipulation suppression, monotonically increasing confidence calibration, and superior discrimination across behavioral scenarios.

Keywords: stress monitoring, multi-signal fusion, behavioral modeling, student wellness, AI academic support, mental health

1. Introduction

1.1 Problem Statement

Mental health challenges among university students have reached epidemic proportions, with 60–80% experiencing significant stress during academic terms [1]. Existing digital wellness platforms rely on single-signal self-reporting, suffering from:

  • Manipulation vulnerability – Students can trivially game single-metric systems
  • Data sparsity – Forgotten check-ins produce no signal despite escalating stress
  • Oscillation noise – Single bad moods can spike scores unnecessarily, triggering false alarms

1.2 Key Contributions

  • A six-signal behavioral stress model with mathematically grounded signal fusion
  • An anti-manipulation defense mechanism using burst detection (22% suppression)
  • A confidence scoring framework quantifying estimation reliability
  • Complete end-to-end platform integrating stress monitoring, AI chatbot, study assistance, grievance management, and parental portals
  • Empirical demonstration of 92% variance reduction and monotonically calibrated confidence

4. Multi-Signal Stress Calculation Engine

4.1 Three-Stage Pipeline

Stage 1: Signal Extraction

Ŝ_t = Σ (w_i* × x_i) Six signals: • Mood (35%) - Self-reported emotion • Sentiment (25%) - NLP analysis of chats • Activity (15%) - Yerkes-Dodson curve • Volatility (10%) - Mood variance (48h) • Time Bias (5%) - IST sleep pattern • Trend (10%) - 7-day momentum

Stage 2: EMA Smoothing

S_t^ema = α × S_{t-1} + (1 - α) × Ŝ_t α = 0.6 (normal) or 0.3 (sparse mode) Result: 96% variance reduction

Stage 3: Logistic Compression

S_final = 100 / (1 + e^(-0.08(S_ema - 50))) Properties: • Soft ceiling/floor behavior • Mid-range amplification for discrimination • Monotonicity preserves ordinal ranking

4.2 Adaptive Weight Redistribution

When signal i lacks data, weight redistributed proportionally to prevent phantom neutral bias:

w_i* = w_i + (w_i / Σw_j) × Σw_k Guarantees: Σw_i* = 1 always

4.3 Anti-Manipulation Defense

Threat: Student sends ≥4 messages with extreme sentiment in <300 seconds.

When burst detected: w_chat,i = e^(-0.15i) × √((i+1)/4) Multipliers: {0.50, 0.71, 0.87, 1.00} Result: 22% sentiment manipulation suppression

4.4 Confidence Scoring

C = (|D|/n × 0.4) + (min(r,10)/10 × 0.35) + max(0.05, 0.25 - σ_x/30 × 0.20) Calibration: 0.15 (zero data) → 0.77 (full data) Span: 0.62 (monotonic increase)

9. Limitations & Future Work

9.1 Limitations

  • Rule-Based by Design – No ML. Intentional for transparency in academic contexts.
  • EMA Lag – Genuine rapid improvement delayed by 1–2 readings. Acceptable trade-off.
  • Timezone Assumption – Hardcoded to IST (+5:30).
  • Keyword-Based NLP – Trades recall for simplicity and no GPU costs.
  • No Clinical Validation – Behavioral approximation, not diagnostic instrument.

9.2 Future Enhancements

10. Conclusion

AURA demonstrates that multi-signal behavioral monitoring provides measurably superior student stress estimation and support compared to single-metric platforms. Through adaptive weight redistribution, the framework gracefully handles data sparsity without phantom anchoring. EMA stabilization reduces oscillation by 96% while maintaining crisis sensitivity.

Logistic compression achieves 92% total variance reduction with soft ceiling/floor behavior and amplified mid-range discrimination. Anti-manipulation burst detection suppresses sentiment gaming by 22%. The three-factor confidence score (span = 0.62) provides calibrated uncertainty quantification enabling appropriate UI communication.

Integrated with AI mental health chatbot, multimodal study assistant, grievance management, and parental portals, AURA forms a complete institutional wellness ecosystem. The transparent, rule-based framework is defensible under academic scrutiny and suitable for institutional contexts where algorithmic accountability is required.
